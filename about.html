About TALL

Rooted in Ottawa’s entertainment scene.

Not a directory. Not a booking app. A person, a network, and a standard.

Meet Christopher Barnett

With years of experience in Ottawa’s entertainment and events scene, Christopher has been part of it all — from high-energy nightclub events and live entertainment to private celebrations, corporate functions and elevated social gatherings.

Through that experience he built strong industry connections, and a real understanding of what makes an event memorable — and what makes an artist ready for one. He created TALL Talent Agency to bring that experience and network together in one place: connecting clients with the right talent to bring their vision to life, and giving talented people a proper way in.

Whether you are planning an intimate gathering, an upscale private event or a large-scale production, the promise is the same: finding the right talent should be simple, and the talent should be worth finding.

Why two sides

Management builds artists.
The agency books them.

Management

For artists on the way up

Direction, input on songs and sound, branding help and career planning — hands-on management until an artist is ready to graduate.

The agency

For artists already there

Established artists with their own manager join the agency side, where the whole focus is bookings: bigger gigs, more exposure, real outreach.

Kept separate

On purpose

The two worlds stay a little separate — for legal reasons, and for artist protection. That separation is a feature, not fine print.
