Questions

Before you ask

What is the difference between Management and the Agency?

Management is for artists still building — direction, input on songs and sound, branding and a career plan, hands-on until you graduate. The Agency is for established artists who already have a manager — it is bookings only: bigger gigs, more exposure, real outreach. The two sides are kept separate on purpose, for legal reasons and to protect artists.

How do I book talent for an event?

Start on the talent pages to see the disciplines, then send the booking form with your date, location and what you are after. TALL matches the right talent to your room and crowd and confirms every detail with you before anything is locked in.

Is the roster full yet?

TALL launched in November 2026 and the founding roster is being signed now. Every artist is reviewed and approved before they are listed, and the first names will be announced on the talent pages as they are confirmed. In the meantime, bookings are matched from TALL’s wider network.

How do I join TALL?

Apply on the Join page and pick your door honestly — Management if you are building, the Agency if you are established, or “not sure” and TALL will place you. Every application is reviewed personally and nothing is published without your approval.

Where does TALL operate?

TALL is based in Ottawa, Ontario, and works with talent and events across Ontario.
