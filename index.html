How TALL works

Two doors into TALL.
Pick the one that fits where you are.

A lot of artists get this wrong, so here it is plainly: management and the agency are two different things, and at TALL they are kept separate on purpose — legally, and to protect the artists.

Door one

TALL Management

For artists still building

Not there yet? Start here. Management is hands-on and personal: direction, honest input on your songs and your sound, help with branding, and a plan for what comes next. Chris manages you until you are ready to graduate.

  • Direction and creative input
  • Branding and image
  • Career planning, step by step
  • A real path to the agency roster
Apply for management
Door two

The Talent Agency

For established artists

Already established, with your own manager? Then the agency side is about one thing: bookings. Bigger rooms, better gigs, and someone reaching out to venues and promoters on your behalf while you focus on the work.

  • Bookings and negotiation
  • Outreach to bigger gigs
  • More exposure, more dates
  • Kept separate from management — deliberately
Apply to the agency

Either way, it starts the same way: you reach out, or we find you. Apply below, tell us honestly where you are, and we will put you at the right door.

About TALL

Run by someone who has actually been in the room.

TALL was founded by Christopher Barnett after years in Ottawa’s entertainment and events scene — high-energy nightclub events, live entertainment, private celebrations, corporate functions and elevated social gatherings.

That experience built a real network, and a simple conviction: great talent and great opportunities should find each other more easily. TALL exists to make that introduction, and to stand behind it.
