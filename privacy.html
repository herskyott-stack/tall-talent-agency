Privacy policy.

Last updated November 8, 2026.

Who we are

TALL Talent Agency is a talent agency and management company based in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada, founded by Christopher Barnett. The person responsible for personal information at TALL is Christopher Barnett, reachable at talltalentagency@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

When you send a booking inquiry, an artist application or a general message, we receive the information you put in it — typically your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, and the details of your event or application. We use it only to respond to you, evaluate applications, and arrange bookings. Artist applications are reviewed privately and are never published without the artist’s approval.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not add people who contact us to a marketing list. This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

Sharing

When a booking is arranged, the details needed to perform it (such as the event date, location and contact person) are shared with the booked artist, and the artist’s contact details may be shared with the client — only as needed for that booking.

Keeping and correcting information

Inquiries and applications are kept only as long as needed for the purpose they were sent. You can ask at any time what information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing talltalentagency@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
